# XIAOLAN / 小岚 · Character Specification

## Status

- `confirmed`
- Fictional character; the source portrait was generated for this research demo.
- Confirmed reference: `xiaolan-character-reference-clean.png`

## Fixed identity anchors

- East Asian woman with an oval face and a calm, focused expression.
- Short straight black bob with one cobalt-blue streak above the right temple.
- Round black glasses.
- Mustard-yellow open utility overshirt over a cream T-shirt.
- Navy cargo trousers and white sneakers.
- Coral-red crossbody satchel worn from the left shoulder to the right hip.
- Cobalt-blue notebook may appear when the task calls for it.

## Allowed variation

- Pose, gaze direction and restrained facial expression.
- Research-related tools and objects.
- Front, side and three-quarter camera angles.
- Actions required by the article's cognitive anchor.

## Not allowed

- Changing hair length, blue-streak position, glasses shape or clothing palette.
- Mirroring the satchel orientation without review.
- Replacing the character with a generic look-alike.
- Adding brand logos, long in-image text or a complete room background.
