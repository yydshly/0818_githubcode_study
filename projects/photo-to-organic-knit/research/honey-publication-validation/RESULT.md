## Research meaning

Tea and honey now share the same template and renderer but use different reviewed Key Art and copy files. This is evidence of cross-art reuse for two selected product examples; it is not a production success-rate claim.

## Boundary

`山野蜜坊`, dates, CTA and product language are sample copy. The result does not verify product origin, nutrition, price, health claims, trademarks, label law, font licensing or commercial publication approval.
