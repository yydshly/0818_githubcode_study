## Case 01 — Family memory × Organic knit

### Source photo

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: source photograph for a family memory book visual transformation demo
Primary request: an elderly East Asian grandmother and her eight-year-old granddaughter sitting close together on a simple wooden porch bench, reading one open picture book, with a relaxed orange cat curled beside them
Scene/backdrop: modest home porch with one broad curved wooden arch framing the pair, a few potted plants, soft garden beyond
Subject: affectionate but natural relationship; grandmother and child both looking at the same book; full upper bodies and hands visible; cat clearly recognizable
Style/medium: photorealistic candid family editorial photography, real skin texture, ordinary clothing, believable wood and fabric
Composition/framing: landscape 3:2, medium-wide eye-level frame; the curved porch arch creates a shelter shape around the three figures
Lighting/mood: warm late-afternoon window light, quiet, safe, intimate, not posed
Color palette: warm wood, cream, muted coral, sage, orange cat
Constraints: no text, no logos, no watermark, no extra people, no holiday decorations, no fantasy styling, no excessive background clutter
```

### Organic knit result

```text
Use case: style-transfer
Asset type: wordless family memory-book cover and children's storybook opening illustration
Input images: Image 1 is the family subject reference. Image 2 is a tactile knit quality reference only; do not copy its train, bridge, landscape, palette, or caption.
Scenario goal: preserve the feeling of intergenerational closeness and shared reading, with enough negative space for a designer to add a title later.
Retain: the recognizable relationship of grandmother and granddaughter leaning together over one open book; the orange cat beside them; the broad curved porch arch as a shelter silhouette; coral vest, sage dress, warm wood.
Transform: turn the arch into one soft oversized knitted embrace loop around the figures; make the open book a small layered felt path that visually connects their hands; simplify the garden into three separated crochet leaf islands; make the cat a compact boucle form.
Discard: realistic house details, door hardware, photographic garden depth, window reflections, continuous background and tiny book illustrations.
Concept: two generations and one cat held inside the same handmade story.
Recomposition: change hierarchy, scale, spacing, continuity and depth. Enlarge the shelter arch, keep the three subjects as one compact emblem, separate secondary elements with warm-ivory gaps, and leave generous editorial margin.
Materials: real crochet, chunky knit, needle felt, boucle, mohair fibers, visible stitches, relaxed edges, sparse loose yarn ends; photographed physical fiber artwork in soft daylight.
Composition: landscape 3:2; complete tactile vignette occupies about 65% of canvas; no cropping; usable warm-ivory space above and around it.
Constraints: no text, no logo, no watermark, exactly two people and one orange cat, readable affectionate poses, no train or bridge.
Avoid: literal wool-filter portrait, copied photo staging, plastic/clay render, perfect symmetry, extra characters, distorted hands dominating the image, detailed realistic background.
```

## Case 03 — Community impact × Stained glass

### Source photo

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: source photograph for a community impact report visual transformation demo
Primary request: five community volunteers gathered in a loose circle around a newly planted young tree in a clean urban courtyard, each person supporting the root ball, soil, watering can, or wooden stake
Scene/backdrop: modest neighborhood courtyard with pale brick ground and a few simple apartment facades kept soft and secondary
Subject: diverse adults seen from a gently elevated angle; hands and shared circular action are more important than individual faces; one vivid green sapling at the center
Style/medium: photorealistic documentary editorial photography, natural clothing and honest textures
Composition/framing: landscape 3:2, gently elevated three-quarter view; people and hands form an open protective circle around the tree; clear central radial structure
Lighting/mood: bright overcast daylight, hopeful, collective, grounded
Color palette: neutral earth, soft blue and rust clothing, fresh green leaves
Constraints: no text, no logos, no watermark, no uniforms, no political symbols, no extra crowd, no staged stock-photo smiles
```

### Stained-glass result

```text
Use case: style-transfer
Asset type: wordless community impact-report cover and public-benefit campaign key visual
Input image: Image 1 is the subject and semantic reference only.
Scenario goal: communicate shared care, diversity and long-term growth with a strong symbol readable at report-cover size.
Retain: five volunteers forming an open circle; their different clothing colors; hands supporting soil, water and stake; one vivid young green tree at the center.
Transform: reinterpret the scene as an original handcrafted stained-glass panel. Turn the five people into five distinct but simplified translucent color fields arranged as a protective ring; make their arms and hands converging lead lines; make the sapling a luminous emerald central axis; turn the watering can and soil into restrained blue and amber glass shapes.
Discard: apartment facades, courtyard paving, realistic faces, photographic clothing folds, incidental tools and continuous background.
Concept: many hands become one living canopy.
Recomposition: change viewpoint, hierarchy, spacing, continuity and silhouette. Use a gently radial composition with an intentional open gap suggesting invitation; tree is tallest focal axis; the circle reads before individual people.
Materials: real hand-cut stained glass, dark lead came, subtle solder joints, bubbles, waviness, opalescent variation and transmitted daylight; contemporary civic craft, not religious iconography.
Composition: landscape 3:2 rectangular panel fully visible in a slim dark frame with quiet neutral surround and safe margin for later report typography.
Color: emerald leaves, blue, rust, amber, cream and smoky neutral glass.
Constraints: no text, no logo, no watermark, exactly five human color figures around one tree, no political or religious symbols.
Avoid: photorealistic faces, corporate stock-photo look, neon cyberpunk, flat vector art, extra crowd, decorative unrelated symbols.
```

## Case 04 — Independent bakery × Layered paper cut

### Source photo

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: source photograph for an independent bakery seasonal campaign transformation demo
Primary request: a small independent neighborhood bakery at opening time, with one baker in a white apron arranging three loaves and a tray of golden pastries in a large arched street-facing window
Scene/backdrop: simple warm stone storefront, striped fabric awning without lettering, one small bicycle leaning nearby, quiet morning street
Subject: the arched window, baker silhouette, repeated bread shapes, awning rhythm and warm interior glow are the defining anchors
Style/medium: photorealistic editorial small-business photography, authentic bread crust, worn stone, cotton fabric and glass reflections
Composition/framing: landscape 3:2, straight-on wide storefront view with generous wall space around the arched window
Lighting/mood: early morning sunlight, welcoming, crafted, local, understated
Color palette: warm cream stone, terracotta, butter gold, muted blue awning
Constraints: no text, no shop name, no logo, no watermark, no customers, no chain-brand styling, no excessive props
```

### Layered paper result

```text
Use case: style-transfer
Asset type: wordless seasonal campaign key visual for an independent neighborhood bakery
Input image: Image 1 is the subject and semantic reference only.
Scenario goal: communicate local craft, morning welcome and repeated handmade bread forms while reserving space for campaign copy added later by a designer.
Retain: the large arched shop window; one baker in a white apron; repeated round loaves and pastry tray; blue-and-cream striped awning; warm stone wall; one bicycle as a small neighborhood anchor.
Transform: rebuild the storefront as a sophisticated layered paper-cut editorial artwork. Make the arched window an oversized warm paper sun/oven portal; reduce the baker to a clear layered silhouette; turn the loaves into rhythmic embossed paper circles and the awning into folded alternating paper bands; simplify the bicycle to one delicate cut-paper line motif.
Discard: glass reflections, interior shelves, realistic stone noise, pavement detail, tree shadow clutter and continuous photographic depth.
Concept: every morning the neighborhood opens through one warm handmade window.
Recomposition: change hierarchy, scale, spacing, continuity and depth. Enlarge the arch, compress secondary architecture into three paper planes, arrange bread as a deliberate rhythm, and leave broad cream wall space for brand copy.
Materials: photographed physical paper sculpture, thick cotton paper, deckled edges, subtle embossing, lifted layers, tiny cutting irregularities and believable cast shadows; refined campaign art.
Composition: landscape 3:2; complete paper vignette and generous clean cream margin; nothing cropped.
Color: cream stone, terracotta, butter gold, muted blue, warm white apron.
Constraints: no text, no shop name, no logo, no watermark, exactly one baker, one bicycle, no customers.
Avoid: childlike craft, flat vector style, plastic 3D, realistic storefront reconstruction, excessive props, unrelated food or lettering.
```

## Why the images contain no final copy

这些场景结果全部采用无字输出：AI 图片负责意境、结构和材料，品牌名、日期、CTA、价格与法律信息由设计师在 Figma、Photoshop 或排版系统中完成。这样更可靠，也更接近真实生产流程。

Case 02 的旅行源图和木刻 Prompt 已分别归档在 `PROMPTS.md` 与 `MULTI_EFFECT_PROMPTS.md`。
