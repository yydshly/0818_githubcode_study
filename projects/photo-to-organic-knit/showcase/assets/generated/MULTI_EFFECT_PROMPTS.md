## 1. Layered paper cut

```text
Use case: style-transfer
Asset type: material-effect demonstration for a public web research showcase
Input image: Image 1 is the subject and semantic reference only.
Shared essence: a lone traveler follows one continuous path through an open mountain gate; calm, solitary forward movement.
Retain: one deep-red canoe, one paddler in a mustard-yellow jacket, the S-shaped route, layered mountain silhouette, sparse autumn reeds.
Transform: rebuild the entire scene as a sophisticated handmade layered paper-cut editorial artwork. Make the river one sweeping cut-paper ribbon with visible deckled edges; turn mountains into five asymmetric overlapping paper planes; turn mist into two translucent torn vellum strips; simplify reeds into sparse folded-paper fringe.
Recomposition: change hierarchy, depth, spacing, continuity, and silhouette. The ribbon path is dominant, the canoe is a small emotional focal point, and broad warm-ivory negative space separates the paper islands. Do not trace the photographic background.
Style/material: photographed physical paper sculpture, thick cotton paper, handmade fibers, tiny scissor irregularities, subtle lifted edges and realistic cast shadows; refined editorial craft, not children's construction paper.
Composition: landscape 3:2 orientation; compact emblem occupies about 65% of canvas; nothing cropped; generous neutral margin.
Color: muted slate blue, reed gold, warm ivory, deep red canoe, mustard jacket.
Constraints: no text, no logo, no watermark, no extra people, no buildings, no continuous realistic landscape.
Avoid: wool, knit, crochet, clay, plastic, digital vector flatness, photorealistic water, perfect symmetry, copied photographic staging.
```
